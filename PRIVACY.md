# Privacy Policy — Smart Village Marketplace

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Smart Village Marketplace is a WhatsApp shopping service for handmade products, run by **DHAPU DEVI CREATIONS** ("we"). This page explains what information the service keeps when you chat with our WhatsApp number, why, and what you can do about it.

Contact for any privacy question or request: **onlineretailshop9@gmail.com**

## What we collect

**When you message us (everyone)**
- Your WhatsApp phone number and the profile name WhatsApp shares with us
- The language you choose
- The messages you send to the service, so the bot can answer them. Message IDs are kept for 7 days only, to avoid answering the same message twice

**When you shop (customers)**
- Your cart, your orders and their status
- The delivery address you send us
- The UPI transaction number you send with the PAID command, so the seller can find your payment

**When you sell (sellers)**
- Your shop name
- Your products: name, price, stock and the product photo you send
- Your UPI ID, and the photo of your UPI QR code if you send one

## What we do not collect

- We never ask for or store bank account numbers, card numbers, UPI PINs or passwords. **Never send these in the chat.**
- We do not handle your money. Customers pay sellers directly through their own UPI app; the payment goes from the customer's bank to the seller's UPI ID.

## How we use your information

- To show products, take orders and send order and payment updates
- To give the seller of your order what they need to deliver it: your name, phone number, delivery address and the items ordered
- To show customers a seller's shop name, UPI ID and QR code so they can pay
- To check new products (handmade products only) and to keep the marketplace safe, for example by blocking sellers who break the rules

We do not sell your information and do not use it for advertising.

## Who can see your information

- **The seller** of an order sees the order details listed above.
- **Marketplace admins** can see sellers, products, orders and payment status to run the service.
- **WhatsApp (Meta)** carries the messages between you and our service, under WhatsApp's own terms and privacy policy.
- We may share information if the law requires it.

## How long we keep it

We keep account, product and order information while the service is running, so you can see your orders and sellers can deliver them. You can ask us to delete your information at any time (see below). We may keep order records that we are required to keep by law.

## Your choices

- Send a message to change your language (LANG) or delivery address (ADDRESS).
- To see, correct or delete your information, email **onlineretailshop9@gmail.com** from any address and include your WhatsApp number. We will reply within 30 days.
- You can stop using the service at any time by not messaging us.

## Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top.
